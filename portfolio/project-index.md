## Why this is useful to a visitor

quality assurance, data checks, automation, and defensive security. The additional and
historical entries show a real learning path without inflating their current maturity.

All private repositories remain private. Their public presentation here contains no source
code, credentials, customer data, deployment addresses, or real scan or migration results.
